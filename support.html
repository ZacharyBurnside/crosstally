Crosstally Support

Need help or found a problem? Email zackburnside@ymail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We read every message.

How do I play?

Every letter is worth its place in the alphabet: A is 1, B is 2, up to Z at 26. Some answers have a normal clue. The rest show only a number, the total of their letters. Fill in the clued words first, then use the letters you have and the totals to work out the rest. Each puzzle has exactly one solution.

Why does a puzzle say “Offline puzzle set”?

The app could not download that day’s puzzles, so it is showing one of its built-in puzzles. Connect to the internet and reopen the app to get the real daily set.

I can’t go back to an older puzzle

The app keeps the last week of puzzles. Older days are not available.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads and no purchases.
