Crosstally Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

You can play Crosstally without an account, and then we collect nothing about you. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, we store a small amount of data to run your stats and the leaderboards, described below. There are no ads and no tracking.

If you play as a guest

Your progress and stats are saved on your phone only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple gives us a random identifier that Apple creates for this app. We do not ask for your name or your email address. We then store:

  • That identifier, so we can recognize your account
  • A leaderboard name that we generate for you, such as “SwiftOtter42”. You cannot type your own name. You can get a new generated name or hide yourself from the leaderboards at any time
  • For each puzzle you finish: the date, level, time, and how many hints and checks you used

We use this only to show your stats and streaks, and to show the daily leaderboards. Other players can see your generated name and your times on the leaderboards. We do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other apps and websites.

Deleting your account

In the app, open the ••• menu, choose Account, and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all results linked to it from our server.

What the app downloads

To get each day’s puzzles, the app downloads puzzle files from our server. Like any web request, this lets the server see standard technical information such as your IP address, which is kept in routine server logs and is not linked to your account or used for anything else.

Information we do not collect

  • Your real name or email address
  • Your contacts
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash tracking

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. We do not receive any information about it.

Children

Crosstally does not ask for your name, email, or other personal details, including from children. Accounts only store the data listed above.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this policy before the change takes effect, and the date above will change.
