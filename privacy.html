Crosstally Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

Crosstally does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking in the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress, such as which puzzles you have finished, is saved on your phone only. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app downloads

To get each day’s puzzles, the app downloads puzzle files from a public web address hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website request, this lets the host see standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not receive that information, and we do not combine it with anything about you. GitHub’s own privacy statement describes how it handles such information.

Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or contacts
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash tracking

Children

Crosstally does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds optional accounts or other features that handle personal information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
