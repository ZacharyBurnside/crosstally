Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The daily puzzles are free to play.

Can I play past days?

You can go back a week, so a missed day is not lost.

Do I need an account?

No. Play as a guest and your stats stay on your phone. Sign in with Apple is optional. It saves your results and puts you on the leaderboards under a generated name.

Why did my solve not count for the leaderboard?

Only puzzles solved without hints are ranked. Checking your letters is fine.

When do new puzzles arrive?

Four new puzzles arrive every day at midnight US Eastern. Turn on the daily reminder in Settings if you want a nudge at 8 AM.

What data do you collect?

None if you play as a guest. If you sign in, we store an anonymous Apple ID, your generated name and your solve times. You can delete your account in the app. Read the privacy policy.
