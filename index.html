How it works

Two kinds of answers in one grid

ABC

Clued answers

A normal crossword clue, like "Footwear (4)". Fill these first to get letters.

23

Number-only answers

No clue. You get the letter total and the length. WOUND is 23+15+21+14+4 = 77.

✓

One solution

Every puzzle is checked by a solver and has exactly one correct fill. No guessing at the end.

Try it

Add up any word

Type a word to see its total, the same arithmetic you use in the game.
